Total straight fertilizer — kg
Why split it: phosphorus and potash are applied fully as basal because they don't move or leach much in the soil. Nitrogen is split because urea applied all at once is easily lost to leaching and volatilisation — giving it in stages, timed to when the crop is actually taking up nitrogen, means more of it ends up in the plant instead of the air or groundwater.

Standard doses shown here are general reference figures compiled from Nepal's agricultural extension guidance and published agronomy trials, meant as a starting point — not a substitute for a soil test or advice from your local agriculture office. Fertilizer composition used: urea 46% N, DAP 18% N + 46% P₂O₅, SSP 16% P₂O₅, TSP 46% P₂O₅, muriate of potash (MOP) 60% K₂O.

Area conversions: 1 hectare = 10,000 m² = 1.4766 bigha = 19.66 ropani. 1 bigha = 20 kattha = 6,772.63 m². 1 ropani = 16 aana = 508.72 m².
